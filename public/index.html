Catch plan-data errors at intake, before they reach go-live.

Paste or upload a plan document. The tool extracts plan fields, links each one to its source line, and gives the reviewer a short checklist with a clear disposition.

    Why this, for Euphoric
    
      
    Euphoric's platform needs clean plan data. This concept checks the documents that data comes from.
    
      
    Euphoric's published Forward-Deployed AI Product Associate role covers document analysis, extraction and data input, AI evaluation, and client implementation. Benefits Intake QA is a small, testable slice of that work. It turns a plan document into fields with evidence, runs consistency checks, and gives the reviewer a go/no-go.
    
      
    The specific pain around PDF intake is my hypothesis. It is not verified with Euphoric or its clients. See the PRD.
