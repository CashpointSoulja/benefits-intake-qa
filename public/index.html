Catch plan-data errors at intake, before they reach go-live.

Paste or upload a plan document. The tool extracts plan fields, links each one to its source line, and gives the reviewer a short checklist with a clear disposition.

Independent prototype by Ayo Ahmed. Not Euphoric's product, not endorsed by Euphoric, and not built for any client. The visual style is inspired by Euphoric's public site. All sample data is synthetic, so please don't upload real documents or personal data.
